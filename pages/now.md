# Now

I've seen a lot of other websites with "Now" pages and I really like the idea. Having my Halloween party be completed is a huge weight off my shoulders, I'm exhausted in a good way. My movie is posted (in case you missed the 10 other places I said this) and trying to get eyes on my work has motivated me to make this site better.

Words can't properly express how healing it was to my soul to see all my friends after a near-year of near-solitude. For all my self-reflection and journaling I'm not really sure if I'm happy. I think that's because my ideal circumstance is very humble, being somewhere quiet or making something or seeing a loved one. The big extravegant thrills, highs and lows of life etc., aren't for me so maybe thats why I feel like I'm always in this grey area. Alternate idea: I need to sleep for a week!

Now that I have no enormous projects looming over me I feel that it is crucial I start learning to draw. It is time. Not being able to draw is my largest insecurity, and the main thing that makes me think I am a fraud. Also as long as I'm updating my website I should start on making a tiny Godot game to embed in my site.

Newsletter send. Murder at Loveland Harbor blog posted. All is right in the world.
